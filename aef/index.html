SELECTED WORK / GEOSPATIAL VISUALISATION

Ars Electronica Festival.

An interactive view of Earth, space research and activity on the ground.

Event
Ars Electronica Festival 2016
Developed at
Ars Electronica Solutions
My role
Interactive visualisation development

Context

For Ars Electronica Festival 2016, I worked on interactive installations presenting content from the European Space Agency and organisations including Reporters, Engineers, Architects and Doctors Without Borders.

Approach

A 4K projection wall controlled by a touch terminal let visitors explore a simulation of Earth, ESA satellite models, high-resolution planetary images and points of interest from space research. A second 4K touch screen focused on geodata and the participating organisations’ field activities.

The experience in detail.

IMAGE GALLERY

Impact

The installations brought space research and field activity into an explorable format. GeoStage, Ars Electronica Solutions’ geovisualisation system, processed heat maps, points of interest, media and text for the interactive displays.

My contribution

I developed interactive installations and geospatial visualisations as part of the Ars Electronica Solutions team. My work supported the displays through which visitors explored space research and the participating organisations' activities.

Technologies
C# · Unity 3D · QGIS · Global Mapper

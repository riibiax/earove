Anshar Online.

A multiplayer space adventure built for virtual reality.

Studio
OZWE Games
Format
Multiplayer VR space shooter
My role
Graphics programming, cinematics & game development

Context

In 2017, I returned to Switzerland and joined OZWE Games to develop VR games. Anshar Online is a multiplayer space shooter with more than 50 missions, contracts and assaults, combining cooperative battles with races and eight-player deathmatches.

Approach

I first designed and implemented cinematics, then continued as a graphics programmer focused on shaders and performance. I also worked on game and level design and created the game trailer. Players progress through loot drops, resources, ship upgrades and class changes.

Impact

The project connected cinematic direction, real-time graphics and gameplay within a commercial VR title. Its recognition included a bronze Best VR Game award at VRDays Europe 2018 and a gold Best Mobile Game award at the Indigo Award 2019, alongside the nominations shown below.

Recognition

  • "Best VR Game" Nominated at the Indie Prize London - Casual Connect Europe 2018

  • "Best VR Game" Winner Bronze at the Halo Awards - VRDays Europe 2018

  • "Best VR Game" Nominated at the 15th IMGA San Francisco 2019

  • "Best Mobile Game" Winner Gold category at the Indigo Award 2019

  • "Best Swiss Game" Nominated at the Ludicious & SGDA Game Awards 2019

My contribution

Cinematic design and implementation, graphics programming, shader development, performance optimisation, game development, level design and production of the game trailer.

Technologies
C# · Shaders · Unity 3D · Blender

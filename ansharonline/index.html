Anshar Online.

A multiplayer space adventure built for virtual reality.

Studio
OZWE Games
Format
Multiplayer VR space shooter
My role
Graphics programming, cinematics & game development

Context

Developed by OZWE Games, Anshar Online is a VR space shooter set around an investigation into the death of the player's partner, Rush Steel. The adventure spans more than 50 missions, from contracts and assaults to space races, with solo play and cooperative missions for up to five players.

Approach

Players fly through asteroid fields, alien worlds and vast spacecraft, steering with head movements and fighting with missiles and lasers. Cross-platform multiplayer brings Oculus Rift, Oculus Go and Samsung Gear VR players into the same universe, including eight-player deathmatches. Loot and experience let players develop their ships across Fighter, Tank and Engineer classes.

Impact

The project connected cinematic direction, real-time graphics and gameplay within a commercial VR title. Its recognition included a bronze Best VR Game award at VRDays Europe 2018 and a gold Best Mobile Game award at the Indigo Award 2019, alongside the nominations shown below.

Recognition

  • "Best VR Game" Nominated at the Indie Prize London - Casual Connect Europe 2018

  • "Best VR Game" Winner Bronze at the Halo Awards - VRDays Europe 2018

  • "Best VR Game" Nominated at the 15th IMGA San Francisco 2019

  • "Best Mobile Game" Winner Gold category at the Indigo Award 2019

  • "Best Swiss Game" Nominated at the Ludicious & SGDA Game Awards 2019

My contribution

I joined OZWE Games in 2017 and began by designing and implementing the game's cinematics. My role then expanded into graphics programming, where I developed shaders and optimised rendering performance for VR. I also contributed to gameplay and level design and created the game trailer, connecting the in-game experience with its presentation to players.

Technologies
C++ · C# · Unity · ShaderLab · Oculus SDK · RenderDoc

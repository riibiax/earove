PROJECT ARCHIVE / DESIGN RESEARCH

Advancing Neurofeedback in Tinnitus.

Making brain activity visible in real time.

Format
Research project
Institution
EPFL+ECAL Lab
My role
Lead developer & visual creation

Context

Tinnitus is the perception of sound without an external source. ANT brought together design, neuroscience and psychology to study how neurofeedback training could be adapted for people with tinnitus.

Approach

During training, real-time visual feedback reflects changes in measured brain activity. For example, changes in alpha activity can make a pattern expand or alter its speed. The team explored how these visual cues affect understanding and engagement.

The experience in detail.

IMAGE GALLERY

Impact

The research informed a neurofeedback protocol that entered clinical testing at University Hospital Zurich in January 2026.

My contribution

I led software development, created the feedback visuals and was the primary developer of the Python library behind them. It uses py5 to render animations from live EEG and can replay recorded signals to test the same visual presets.

Technologies

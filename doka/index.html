SELECTED WORK / INTERACTIVE DATA WALLS

Doka at BAUMA.

A global network of construction projects, explored through touch.

Client
Doka Group
Developed at
Ars Electronica Solutions
My role
GeoStage software development

Context

For Doka Group’s booth at BAUMA, the international construction and mining machinery fair, Ars Electronica Solutions created two interactive Reference Walls to present the company’s worldwide network through georeferenced data.

Approach

Each wall combined 12 monitors with animated effects to attract visitors. A globe formed the main interface: visitors could rotate it, zoom in and select locations to explore videos, photographs, text and statistics. I contributed to the GeoStage software behind the installation.

The experience in detail.

IMAGE GALLERY

Impact

The walls became entrance points to Doka’s exhibition, making its international references explorable and supporting conversations between visitors and the sales team.

My contribution

I contributed to the GeoStage software as part of the Ars Electronica engineering team. The software connected the touch-controlled globe with the photographs, videos and project information presented across the Reference Walls.

Technologies
C# · Unity 3D · TUIO · QGIS · Global Mapper

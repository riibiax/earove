Re-Think the Eames.

An immersive exhibition revisiting the Eames’ multimedia experiments.

School
Haute école d’art et de design, Geneva
Exhibition
Salone del Mobile · Milan 2013
My role
Exhibition concept & interactive wall

Context

For Salone del Mobile in Milan in 2013, students from the Haute école d’art et de design in Geneva revisited educational multimedia spectacles, taking inspiration from Charles and Ray Eames’ 1964 Think Theater.

Approach

We created a multi-screen environment presenting 700 unreleased images from the Eames collection, alongside an interactive wall where visitors could answer questions about design practice and the exhibition. As part of the student design team, I contributed to the concept and realisation of the wall.

The experience in detail.

IMAGE GALLERY

Impact

The exhibition connected the Eames’ archive with contemporary interaction. Answers collected in Milan were displayed in a parallel exhibition in Geneva, extending visitors’ contributions beyond the original space.

My contribution

I helped develop the exhibition concept and realise the interactive wall within the student design team. My contribution brought the shared concept into an installation that invited visitors to engage with the Eames' archive.

Technologies
Max/MSP · Arduino · OpenCV · Adobe Premiere

42 Lausanne.

Making a peer-to-peer learning culture tangible through playful interaction.

Client
42 Lausanne, Renens
Developed at
UDIMU SA
My role
Interaction design & software development

Context

At UDIMU SA, I worked on interactive installations for 42 Lausanne, the computer science school based on peer-to-peer learning. The space needed to communicate the school’s mission and learning culture to students, visitors and staff.

Approach

My role combined design and development, with interactive experiences built in Unity 3D and React. For the inauguration, we also created an augmented reality treasure hunt for iOS and Android using Unity’s AR Foundation framework.

Impact

The installations offered a playful introduction to the school and its learning culture, while the inauguration treasure hunt extended the experience onto visitors’ phones.

My contribution

Interaction design and software development across the installations, including the mobile AR game.

Technologies
C# · Shaders · Unity 3D · AR Foundation · React · Blender

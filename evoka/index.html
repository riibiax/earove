Èvokâ II.

A memory of Fribourg becomes a way into its archives.

Format
Permanent installation · 2026
Location
Cantonal and University Library, Fribourg
My role
Lead frontend developer & interaction design contributor

Context

How do you invite someone into an archive when they have nothing in particular to look for? EPFL+ECAL Lab, BCU Fribourg and the Swiss Data Science Center spent four years exploring how personal memories could open up the library’s digital collections.

Approach

Visitors step into a booth, pick up a handset and share a memory of Fribourg. An AI-guided conversation surfaces related material from the library’s collections, which hold nearly 70,000 images alongside sound recordings, films and newspapers.

The experience in detail.

IMAGE GALLERY

Impact

Unveiled at the 2026 European Heritage Days, Èvokâ II is now a permanent installation at BCU Fribourg. A visitor’s own story can lead them to a piece of local history they might never have thought to look for.

My contribution

I led frontend development in Unity and worked with the team in Figma to design the touchscreen interface and shape how visitors move through the experience. On the backend, I helped integrate a large language model (LLM) for indexing and searching the archive.

Technologies
Unity · Figma · Large language models

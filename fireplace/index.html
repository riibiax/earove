Project
Fireplace

Context

Fireplace is an interactive lumino-kinetic device designed to explore the psychological effects of light and the relationship between technology, perception and cognitive response.

Approach

The project combines a mobile app that records physical activity with a domestic device that plays visual light programmes. Flicker, colour combinations, frequency, intensity and pattern form an experimental visual language based on patterns associated with brain waves.

The experience in detail.

IMAGE GALLERY

Impact

The device provides a setting for experimenting with light and perception, connecting a record of physical activity with changing visual patterns in the home.

Recognition

  • Nominated for the Award of Excellence, Hans Wilsdorf Fondation, 2014.

My contribution

I worked on Fireplace by bringing together software, physical computing and experiments with light. My contribution explored how a domestic object could translate recorded activity into changing visual patterns.

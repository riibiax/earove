KF Watches.

Bringing fine watchmaking into real-time 3D.

Client
KF Watches
Developed at
UDIMU SA
My role
Real-time 3D & interaction development

Context

Created at UDIMU SA, the configurator needed to let clients explore KF Watches’ models through a touch interface, while conveying the quality of the materials and the complexity of each design.

Approach

I developed the experience in Unity HDRP, optimising 3D models, lighting and shaders alongside the interface and animations. Particular care went into the appearance of diamonds and metal surfaces.

The experience in detail.

IMAGE GALLERY

Impact

The resulting configurator brings watch variations into one interactive experience, allowing users to customise individual components and explore colours and materials directly on screen.

My contribution

I developed the real-time rendering and touch interface in Unity HDRP, working on 3D model optimisation, lighting and shaders to convey the watches' materials and detail. I also created cinematic animations that present the watches as users select a model or configure its components.

Technologies
C# · Unity HDRP · Shaders · Blender

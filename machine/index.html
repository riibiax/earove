SELECTED WORK / INTERACTION DESIGN

Machine Enchantée.

A library that becomes a landscape of discovery for children.

Event
UMIX Information Architecture Hackathon
Place / year
Lyon · 2014
My role
Team interaction concept

Context

At the UMIX Information Architecture Hackathon 2014 in Lyon, teams had 48 hours to rethink physical and digital libraries. Our concept used serendipity to help children aged three to seven discover books and games through play.

Approach

A friendly classification system, developed together with children, uses their interests, topics and favourite books. Children select symbols on a touch-screen table, then follow branching LED paths through the library towards special boxes that light up with book and game selections. Up to four users can follow different routes at once.

Impact

Machine Enchantée won the hackathon. The concept turns choosing a book into a physical journey, connecting a digital selection with discovery throughout the library.

My contribution

Team development of the interaction concept for a playful, child-centred library experience.

SELECTED WORK / DATA VISUALISATION

METEC.

Factory data brought into focus through interactive 4K walls.

Client
Primetals Technologies
Developed at
Ars Electronica Solutions
My role
Interactive 4K wall development

Context

For METEC in June 2015, Ars Electronica Solutions created an immersive environment for Primetals Technologies. The exhibition supported the launch of the company’s new name and corporate design at a leading event for metallurgical technology.

Approach

An open exhibition space combined interactive walls, terminals and tables with a central installation of 24 screens, 12 of which could rotate. Inspired by metallurgical processes, the exhibits presented complex information in an intuitive, playful way. I developed the interactive 4K screen walls used to visualise factory data.

Impact

The environment brought technical content and the new corporate identity into one exhibition experience, using data visualisation and interaction to make factory information accessible to visitors.

My contribution

Development of the interactive 4K screen walls for visualising factory data from Primetals Technologies.

Technologies

BeeHive Mons.

A city’s stories collected on phones and explored around an interactive table.

Project
Mons 2015 · Café Europa
Developed at
Ars Electronica Solutions
My role
Contribution to GeoPulse BeeHive Mons

Context

In March 2015, I delivered my first project with Ars Electronica Solutions: GeoPulse BeeHive Mons. At Café Europa in Mons, the European Capital of Culture, visitors could explore video stories from across the city.

Approach

An Android and iOS app let participants record georeferenced video, stored and indexed in a database. An interactive table made the collection browsable by topic or place. Like bees returning to a hive, participants gathered events from different perspectives into a collective pool.

The experience in detail.

IMAGE GALLERY

Impact

The project connected mobile capture with a shared public interface, bringing individual perspectives on the city into a collection that visitors could explore together.

My contribution

I worked on GeoPulse BeeHive Mons with the Ars Electronica Solutions team. My contribution formed part of an experience that connected video stories recorded around the city with a shared interactive table.

Production
A co-production of Ars Electronica and Mons 2015, supported by the Austrian Cultural Forum in Brussels, the Austrian Ministry of Culture and the Austrian Embassy.

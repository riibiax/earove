SELECTED WORK / INTERACTIVE STORYTELLING

Once Upon a Tale.

Familiar stories become a playground for new narrative combinations.

Format
Tactile book & interactive application
Development
Andrea Rovescalli & Praiya Chinagarn
Illustrations
Benjamin Ben Kemoun

Context

Once Upon a Tale is a tactile book and interactive storytelling application based on well-known children’s stories. It invites readers to reinvent familiar tales and build their own combinations.

Approach

Through a card game or touch interface, players select characters and settings from different stories to generate combinations of text and illustration. Stories can be changed at any moment: Cinderella might become the big bad wolf in Bluebeard’s castle.

Impact

The project makes storytelling an activity to shape and remix, connecting physical play with a digital application and illustrations that respond to the chosen narrative.

My contribution

I developed the application with Praiya Chinagarn, bringing the storytelling interactions into a digital experience. We worked with illustrations by Benjamin Ben Kemoun so that readers could combine characters and settings and see their stories take shape on screen.

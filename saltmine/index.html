Berchtesgaden Salt Mine.

Light, image and sound inside a chamber carved from rock.

Venue
Berchtesgaden Salt Mine
Created at
Ars Electronica Solutions
My role
Concept design

Context

The Treasure Chamber is one of the main attractions of the Berchtesgaden Salt Mine’s SaltTimeJourney guided tour. Ars Electronica Solutions redesigned this large underground space carved into the rock.

Approach

Across four projection screens, abstract crystalline animations tell the story of salt through nature, humankind, history and technology. A dedicated soundtrack supports the imagery, while a pulsating salt lamp created by one of the mine’s associates provides another focal point.

The experience in detail.

Impact

Light, image, sound and the underground setting combine into an immersive environment that forms part of the SaltTimeJourney visitor experience.

My contribution

Concept design as part of the Ars Electronica Solutions team.

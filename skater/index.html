The Skater.

A paper city, an iPhone and an augmented reality skate adventure.

Format
Mobile augmented reality game
Design & development
Andrea Rovescalli, Praiya Chinagarn & Solkin Keizer
Setting
Berlin in the 1980s

Context

Imagine your iPhone as a skater moving through circuits and avoiding obstacles. Set in 1980s Berlin, The Skater follows a young character trying to spread American skate culture while staying ahead of the police.

Approach

In a visual world inspired by zine culture, printed cards build each level and physical origami pieces help solve virtual obstacles. The player moves between a handmade paper city and its augmented reality counterpart.

The experience in detail.

IMAGE GALLERY

Impact

The game connects a mobile virtual world with physical cards and origami, making the objects around the screen part of the play experience.

My contribution

I designed and developed the game with Praiya Chinagarn and Solkin Keizer. Together, we explored how printed cards and origami could become part of an augmented reality skate adventure.

Technologies
C++ · Shaders · openFrameworks · Cinema 4D

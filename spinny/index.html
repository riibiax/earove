Spinny’s Journey.

A small alien, a spinning spaceship and a 2D action-puzzle adventure.

Studio
Thunder Sparrow
Platforms
Nintendo Switch & PC
My role
Game development & production

Context

During the Covid lockdown, I developed a game with friends to explore the Nintendo Switch market and the process of creating a company in Switzerland: Thunder Sparrow. Spinny’s Journey follows a tiny alien whose spaceship is destroyed by a terrifying sparrow.

Approach

Inspired by Kuru Kuru Kururin on Game Boy Advance, the gameplay centres on rotation, timing and narrow paths. Players guide Spinny through mazes, avoid traps and repair his spaceship. I worked across game production, from team and supplier coordination to user testing, communication and marketing.

Impact

The project brought together an independent game for Nintendo Switch and PC and hands-on experience of building a studio in Switzerland, connecting creative decisions with the technical and organisational demands of production.

My contribution

I developed gameplay systems, user interfaces and shaders in Unity, optimising their performance for Nintendo Switch and PC. I also trained intelligent agents in Unity environments using deep reinforcement learning and imitation learning. Alongside development, I helped create and manage Thunder Sparrow, developed the business plan and coordinated work with external partners.

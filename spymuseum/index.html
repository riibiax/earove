Spy Museum.

Exploring the history of espionage through interactive experiences.

Institution
Spy Museum, Berlin
Developed at
Ars Electronica Solutions
My role
Contribution to interactive installations

Context

In September 2015, I worked with Ars Electronica Solutions on interactive installations for the Spy Museum in Berlin. Across more than 3,000 square metres, the permanent exhibition tells stories of covert operations, espionage cases and legendary spies.

Approach

The exhibition combines historical objects, documents, photographs, films, interviews and expert testimony with multimedia and museum technology. Interactive experiences make exploration and discovery part of the visit.

The experience in detail.

IMAGE GALLERY

Impact

Historical material and interactive encounters sit together within the permanent exhibition, giving visitors different ways to explore the people, methods and stories behind espionage.

My contribution

I worked on the interactive installations with the Ars Electronica Solutions team. My contribution formed part of the museum's approach to bringing the history of espionage into experiences visitors could explore for themselves.

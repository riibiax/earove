SWITCH Miso.

Five days of conversation about what we eat and why it matters.

Format
Research prototype, 2026
Institution
EPFL+ECAL Lab
My role
Engineering team

Context

What did you eat yesterday, and what shaped that choice? Miso starts with questions like these to make healthy, sustainable eating easier to think about in the context of everyday life. We developed the prototype at EPFL+ECAL Lab as part of the Horizon Europe SWITCH project to explore how conversational AI could help people build food literacy and make more informed choices.

Approach

Miso leads a series of short daily conversations over five days. Each session introduces a topic and invites people to connect it to meals they already eat or dishes they might try. Along the way, participants unlock practical guides, such as a seasonal produce calendar or a guide to reading food labels. The app keeps those guides in an animated toolbox and lets people revisit earlier conversations by day.

Impact

In a five-day field study with 14 participants, people valued the prompts about their own habits, the guided conversations and the short take-home tools. Participants reported learning more about food and described changes they intended to try. The team’s findings were presented at NordiCHI 2026 in Vaasa, Finland.

My contribution

I was part of the engineering team that developed Miso at EPFL+ECAL Lab. We built the mobile-first web app with React, connected the OpenAI-powered chat through Socket.IO, and used MongoDB to save conversations and study progress between visits. I also co-authored the research paper on Miso’s design and evaluation.

SELECTED WORK / GENERATIVE ART

Tabak Fabrik.

Personal memories translated into a generative visual narrative.

Project
Personal generative artwork
Place
Tabak Fabrik, Linz, Austria
My role
Concept, 3D reconstruction & development

Context

Tabak Fabrik is a 12-minute visual piece created from memories of my two years working at Ars Electronica Solutions. The former tobacco factory in Linz became a creative hub and a setting for the places, people and events explored in the work.

Approach

Using photogrammetry, I recreated buildings, spaces, statues and objects as 3D models. I then developed an animation framework driven by data from meaningful moments: emails, web traffic, images and sounds converted into binary code.

Interactive visualization

ORIGINAL THREE.JS ANIMATION

The experience in detail.

IMAGE GALLERY

Impact

The result is an abstract narrative about experiences that remain, turning traces of everyday life into moving visual forms. For privacy reasons, the underlying data is not shown; the gallery presents stills from the visual work.

My contribution

Artistic concept, photogrammetry, 3D reconstruction, data processing, shader work and development of the animation framework.

Technologies
Three.js · Wireshark · GLSL · Agisoft PhotoScan

The Way.

An escape game that makes retirement a topic to explore through play.

Client
Vaudoise Assurances
Developed at
UDIMU SA
My role
Game, interface & tool development

Context

When I joined UDIMU SA in 2021, my first project was an interactive totem for Vaudoise Assurances. The Way introduces retirement through an imaginative escape game: what if you could fast-forward to age 65 in just 45 minutes?

Approach

I developed the mini-games and UI system, together with localisation tools, an Excel import pipeline for Unity diagrams and an assistance system to guide users through the experience.

The experience in detail.

Impact

The totem combines play, visual information and guided interaction to give visitors an accessible way to engage with the topic of retirement.

My contribution

Mini-game development, interface systems, localisation tools, the Excel-to-Unity diagram pipeline and the user assistance system.

Technologies
